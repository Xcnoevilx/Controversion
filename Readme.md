# Controversion

Controversion is an evidence-focused platform for exploring controversial claims, conspiracy theories, disputed events, and competing explanations.

## Purpose

The goal is not to tell people what to believe. Controversion organizes claims, evidence, sources, counterarguments, and alternative explanations so users can examine a subject and reach their own conclusions.

## Core Features

- Browse current and popular controversial topics
- Examine claims and supporting evidence
- View counterarguments and contradictory evidence
- Compare alternative hypotheses
- Source and credibility information
- Community discussion and debate
- User belief/confidence ratings
- User-submitted topics and evidence
- Duplicate-topic detection
- Educational content on misinformation, scams, phishing, deepfakes, and media literacy
- New-topic discovery and research bot

## Guiding Principles

1. Evidence before conclusions
2. Clearly distinguish facts, claims, opinions, and speculation
3. Preserve competing explanations
4. Cite original and reliable sources whenever possible
5. Explain uncertainty rather than hiding it
6. Encourage critical thinking rather than telling users what to believe
7. Correct information when better evidence becomes available

## Status

Controversion is currently in early development.
